The Paperless Landlord

Tech Thriller / Neo-Noir Business Drama

A fictional story grounded in real UK landlord pressures: rent delays, compliance deadlines and daily admin that can overwhelm small operators without reliable systems.

Synopsis

In a rain-soaked city where every missed reminder has a cost, one independent landlord is pushed to breaking point by paper files, scattered spreadsheets and late-night tenant emergencies. As pressure rises from arrears and compliance deadlines, the only path forward is a full shift to disciplined digital operations. The Paperless Landlord explores the tension between chaos and control, and the practical systems needed to protect both tenants and business stability.
